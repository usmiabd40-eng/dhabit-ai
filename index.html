OUR IDENTITY

ضَابِط AI — Dhabit AI

Arabic. Accurate. With Context.

Dhabit AI is an AI-powered Arabic diacritization platform designed to add accurate tashkeel (حركات) to Arabic text using contextual understanding, Arabic grammar (النحو), and morphology (الصرف).

Our Mission

To make correctly vocalized Arabic accessible to everyone through intelligent technology.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Arabic language tool for students, teachers, researchers, writers, and Arabic learners worldwide.

Our Brand Promise

Helping people write and read Arabic with greater clarity and confidence.

Who It’s For

Students, educators, Islamic studies learners, researchers, writers, and Arabic content creators.
